# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to Learners. This glossary fixes the language we use for buying courses and getting money back for them.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
The person who bought a course and watches it.
_Avoid_: User (when the buyer is meant), student, customer

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's request, with their reason, to get the money for a purchase back. It is reviewed by a person and is approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the request itself), cancellation, reversal, chargeback, dispute

**Pending** / **Approved** / **Declined**:
The states of a Refund Request. Pending means it is waiting for a person to review it. Approved means a Refund was given. Declined means no money was returned.
_Avoid_: Requested, open, raised, rejected

**Refund**:
Money returned to a Learner after a Refund Request is approved. An approved Refund also ends the Learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, chargeback, credit

**Refund Amount**:
The amount the Learner actually paid for the purchase. It is never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refunded**:
The state of a purchase whose Refund Request was approved. The record is kept, but the Learner no longer has access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, revoked, reversed

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase. During this time a Learner can submit a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period, grace period

**Refund Eligibility**:
All the rules that decide whether a Learner can submit a Refund Request for a purchase. These are the Refund Window, under 2 hours of the course watched, under half of its lessons completed, and no claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancel rules

**Chargeback**:
A payment reversal forced by the Learner's card issuer after they dispute the charge with their bank. It happens outside our Refund process.
_Avoid_: Using this word for a Refund or a Refund Request
